# W1 Brand, positioning and copy audit

Scope: site/src/i18n.ts, more.ts, extra.ts, App.vue, PlayGate.vue, index.html (EN and TR checked closely; ZH/JA only skimmed).

[W1-1][P0][copy] Beta steps say "No sign-up for now" but the gate says Google sign-in plus a code; the visitor is told two different things — site/src/i18n.ts:132 (TR :238) — replace with "Sign in with Google, pick a name, a look and a pet." / TR "Google ile giriş yap; bir isim, bir görünüm ve bir evcil hayvan seç."
[W1-2][P0][cta] Primary hero button says "Play on the web" but most visitors lack a code and land in a "still building" modal; the promise and the result disagree — i18n.ts:58, App.vue:252,326 — rename to "Join the closed beta" / TR "Betaya katıl", and make the code request the main modal action, not "See the game".
[W1-3][P0][trust] Nowhere says who it is for, that it is free, ad-free, or what data is kept (Google email, chats, study stats); only terms/privacy links in the footer — i18n.ts:54-63, App.vue:603 — add one hero line: "Free. No ads. We only keep your Google email and your game profile." / TR "Ücretsiz. Reklam yok. Yalnızca Google e-postanı ve oyun profilini saklarız." (verify against privacy.html first).
[W1-4][P1][copy] Hero subtitle omits the core idea (quiet, body doubling) and the audience; "smoking room" is the only noun about breaks — i18n.ts:56-57 — "Study quietly beside real people. Chat on your breaks. Slowly, make friends. For students and anyone who works better in company." / TR "Gerçek insanların yanında sessizce çalış, molada sohbet et, yavaş yavaş arkadaş ol. Kalabalıkta daha iyi odaklananlar için."
[W1-5][P1][i18n] TR title "Yalnız çalışma." can be read as the noun phrase "lonely work"; negative imperative is unclear — i18n.ts:161 — "Tek başına çalışma." (or "Yalnız ders çalışma.").
[W1-6][P1][brand] "Smoking room" for a calm, student-friendly break room clashes with the warm, healthy tone and may put off parents/schools; README uses it too — i18n.ts:73,179, extra.ts:32 — consider "break room" / TR "mola odası" on the site, or add a line that it is only a name (no smoking, no minors issue).
[W1-7][P1][cta] Access-code path is vague: "Ask for it" never says where or how fast; email lives only in a mailto — i18n.ts:128, more.ts:27 — "Email us and we usually reply within a day with this week’s code." / TR "Bize yaz; bu haftanın kodunu genellikle bir gün içinde göndeririz." (state the real turnaround).
[W1-10][P2][i18n] TR mixes "Petler" (extra.ts:60 nav) and "Evcil hayvanlar" (i18n.ts:206) — pick "Evcil hayvanlar" in nav too (or "Petler" everywhere).
[W1-13][P2][trust] "Made by Ramazan Doğan" has no face, story or contact; the Japan/friendship "why" from README is absent from the site and is the strongest trust asset — i18n.ts:145, App.vue footer — add a two-sentence founder note: "I built this after seeing how many people in Japan eat and study alone."
[W1-14][P2][brand] App Store / Google Play "Coming soon" badges promise native apps the game may not deliver (CODEMAP shows a web client only) — i18n.ts:59-61, App.vue:332 — remove, or say "Web only for now".
