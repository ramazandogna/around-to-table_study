# T2 Player-experience playtest

Played from the website: (a) 1440x900 EN, (b) iPhone 13 tr-TR, (c) same account as (a), Date +25h. A second context ("Kenji") met (a) in the smoking room. Shots `shots/T2-*.png` (12). Local: Play links go to production workers.dev; no Google button locally. I continued at :8787.

## (a) Lonely student, Japan, desktop: 7/10
- **First 30s:** "Don't study alone", cozy blocky characters, study next to real people. Clear and charming. The "invite-only, ask for this week's access code" line says nothing about where to ask.
- **Lost:** Door signs aren't clickable, so I guessed floor clicks; walking stalled under archways. Pomodoro needs Start… then "Yes, start". The welcome coach reappears in every room.
- **Delighted:** "What will you work on?" choice, pomodoro ring, "session done, well studied!", the break nudge to the smoking room, Kenji's reply bubble, the music player, the quest ticking itself.
- **Won't return:** an empty library ("0 studying") feels like my own desk; nothing sets up a time to meet anyone.

## (b) Turkish user, phone: 6/10
- **First 30s:** Site opened in English despite tr-TR; I found EN > TR.
- **Lost:** The game itself opened in English, create screen and long intro included. The language button is hidden behind "🚪 3 ▾". The coach card covers the joystick.
- **Delighted:** After switching, everything is Turkish; joystick and tap-walk work; seated within about 2 minutes.
- **Won't return:** English first screens; "sigara odası" sounds odd for a student.

## (c) Returning next day: 5/10
- Lobby remembers: Studied 25m, streak 1, today 0m/1h; fresh quests, goals kept.
- No welcome back or recap, first-visit coach shown again, Kenji not remembered or offered as a friend. Only quests give a reason to stay.

## Findings
[T2-1][P1][onboarding] Game ignores site language and browser locale and opens in English — tr-TR + site set to TR, game create screen and intro in EN (b3) — pass the language to the game or read navigator.language.
[T2-2][P1][retention] No welcome-back or progress recap, and the first-visit coach shows again — c2 shot, hall coach after +1 day — show "Welcome back, 25m yesterday, streak N" once and mark the coach seen.
[T2-3][P2][site] "Ask for this week's access code" has no way to ask, and the game never asks for a code — a1 — remove the line or say where the code comes from.
[T2-4][P2][ux] Starting a pomodoro takes Start… then "Yes, start" — a8 — drop the second confirm.
[T2-5][P2][mobile] Language switch hidden under "🚪 3 ▾", and the coach card sits on the joystick — b3, b4 — expose a language button and move the coach.
[T2-6][P2][retention] An empty library gives a lonely player no cue to meet anyone — a6 ("0 studying") — surface "someone is on break in the smoking room".
[T2-7][P3][ux] Door signs aren't tappable and avatars stall under archways — walking attempts in hall — make signs travel targets.
[T2-8][P3][copy] TR headline "Yalnız çalışma." can read as "work alone" — site TR — reword.

## Round 2 (new build)
Replayed with new accounts. Local only: "I have a code: open the game" navigates to production workers.dev?lang=tr (unreachable here); I continued at :8787 (with ?lang=tr, and also with no param under tr-TR). Finals: `shots/final-*.png` (8).

**(a) Desktop, Japan: 8/10 (was 7).** The site now says what the beta is: "Join the closed beta", a modal with "Ask for this week's code", copyable address, "I have a code", "Explore the site", plus a free/no-ads/data line. The access-code mystery is gone. Esc closes the intro and Goals. The coach is one short line ("Tap a glowing chair to sit and study."). After the pomodoro, "Break's over. Ready for round 2? Finish for now / Let's go" walked me back to my chair and started round 2 on its own: the best change. Still missing: pomodoro still needs Start… then "Yes, start"; the library still has no cue that anyone is around; signs still aren't tappable and I stalled twice under the library arch.

**(b) Turkish phone: 8/10 (was 6).** tr-TR browser now opens the site in Turkish, the headline is the clearer "Tek başına çalışma.", the TR modal fits at 390px, and the game opened in Turkish both via ?lang=tr and with no param. The first screen is no longer a wall of English. Still missing: on the create screen "Gir" (Enter) is below the fold at 390x664 and the terms line is cut off; the in-game tagline is still the old ambiguous "Yalnız çalışma."; the hall greeting is now a small bubble from reception that can sit off the top edge on a phone, so I nearly missed it; "sigara odası" unchanged.

**(c) Returning, next day: 7/10 (was 5).** The hall shows "Welcome back · 25m yesterday · streak 1", the first-visit coach does not come back, and quests refresh. Still missing: the line is a small toast that fades fast; no mention of who I met (Kenji), no goal or "continue where you stopped" suggestion, and no hook for today beyond quests and the meetup countdown.
