# A2: Game & Product Design audit

Scope: core loop, retention without dark patterns, warmth vs. quiet, cold start, system health. Read-only pass, 2026-11-17.

## Findings

- [A2-1][P1][focus/profile] The client sets its own timer (`remainingMs: 0`, `totalMs` up to 3 h) and `finished` then credits a full block at once, from any area, seated or not. Badges, focus hours and the profile on everyone's player card can be faked in seconds. Files: `apps/server/src/area-room.ts:897-905`, `:645-653`, `packages/shared/src/protocol.ts:56-57`. Fix: record the block start on the server and credit only the server-measured seated time, capped at `totalMs`.
- [A2-2][P1][core loop] At the end of a break, "Let's go" starts the next work block wherever the player is. Focus time (and goals) then fills while they chat in the smoking room, and nothing walks them back to the desk, so the return step of the loop is missing. Files: `apps/client/src/state/store.ts:1025-1034`, `apps/client/src/ui/TimerHud.tsx:109-120`. Fix: make "Let's go" walk the player back to their chair, and start the block when they sit.
- [A2-3][P1][cold start] A lone first visitor sees no sign of when others come. The only regular event is a Thursday social meetup, and there is no shared study hour, so the few players of a small beta never overlap. Files: `packages/shared/src/events.ts:51-62`, `apps/client/src/ui/CharacterSelect.tsx`. Fix: add a daily "study hour" anchor event in the library, and show "N in the building · next study hour 20:00" before entry.
- [A2-4][P1][friends] Nothing sits between "stranger" and "friend". Friending is instant from a card or a name search, and nothing remembers that you studied next to someone four times, which is the "slowly become friends" promise. File: `apps/server/src/social-hub.ts:889-935`. Fix: record co-presence (same table, overlapping blocks, shared breaks) and show "familiar faces" on cards and the phone.
- [A2-5][P2][retention] Badges reward volume (`chatty`: 100 messages, building shouts included) and idle presence (`regular`: 24 h in the building), which are engagement metrics and not the soul. Files: `packages/shared/src/profile.ts:100-101`, `apps/server/src/social-hub.ts:1384`. Fix: replace them with relational or quiet badges ("studied beside 5 people", "came back from 10 breaks").
- [A2-6][P2][retention] One missed day wipes the streak, and that loss aversion is the classic dark pattern. File: `apps/client/src/state/stats.ts:39-43`. Fix: allow two rest days a week, or count a weekly rhythm.
- [A2-7][P2][daily tasks] Three of nine tasks are picked by date, so some days have no study task and push dancing and chatting instead. File: `apps/client/src/state/daily.ts:38-45`. Fix: always include one focus task and one gentle social task.
- [A2-8][P2][library growth] Strips never close, so after one busy evening the library stays permanently half empty, which is the exact feeling the feature was built to avoid. Files: `packages/shared/src/world/grid.ts:175-180`, `apps/server/src/area-room.ts:857-865`. Fix: see the decision proposals.
- [A2-9][P2][together] "Together" tells the lone visitor "Nobody here is in a focus block", at the moment they most need company. Files: `apps/client/src/i18n/en.json:359`, `packages/shared/src/study.ts:46`. Fix: hide "together" when no block is running, and frame the librarian as quiet company instead.
- [A2-10][P3][staff] Staff talk when tapped but never acknowledge the player (a reception greeting by name, a librarian nod when a block finishes). This is cheap warmth that is missing for an empty server. File: `apps/client/src/world/Staff.ts:246-273`. Fix: add two or three player-aware lines.
- [A2-11][P3][dead feature] Clubs are fully specified but unbuilt, and they overlap private rooms plus friends. File: `docs/decisions.md` ("Clubs (planned)"). Fix: defer them until co-presence (A2-4) exists, since that is what a club should grow from.

## Decision proposals

- Revisit "the library grows and never closes again": let strips re-tape after about 7 quiet days, and keep a plaque or log that the library once grew.
- Add a recurring daily library "study hour" to `EVENTS`, next to the Thursday meetup.
- Replace the `chatty` and `regular` badges with co-presence badges.
- Soften streaks with rest days, consistent with "no rankings; numbers are there to feel good".
