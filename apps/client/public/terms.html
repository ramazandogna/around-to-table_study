What this is

Around the Table is a free, early beta of a shared study building made by Ramazan Doğan. It may change, break or be reset at any time; a reset can remove characters, friends, messages and stats.

Who may use it

You must be 16 or older and sign in with your own Google account; one account is one person, with up to two characters. During the beta, entering the game also needs the current access code, which changes with every update.

Be kind

  • No harassment, hate, threats, sexual content or sharing other people's private information.
  • No spam, ads or selling. Links are not allowed in most places.
  • Rude words are masked or refused; repeated swearing slows your chat down.
  • Use mute and block when someone bothers you. Room owners can send people out of their rooms.

What we may do

We may remove content, end sessions or keep someone out to protect others or the service.

Music and videos

Songs queued in the smoking room play through YouTube's embedded player and YouTube's own terms apply to them. Queue only what you are allowed to share.

No warranty

The beta is provided as it is, without guarantees, as far as the law allows.
